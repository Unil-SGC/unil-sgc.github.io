Data ​

Finding and storing data for geocomputing at FGSE.

  • Geodata catalogues: a curated list of geodata sources, from Swiss 3D models to remote sensing and natural hazards.
  • Shared datasets: large reference datasets of interest to several research groups, hosted on the Curnagl cluster.
  • Storage and transfer: research data storage, long-term storage and large file transfer are DCSR services. They are listed on the Centre's resources page on unil.ch.
