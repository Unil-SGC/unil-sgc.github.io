Geodata catalogues ​

Where to find geodata for Earth and environmental sciences: Swiss portals and elevation models, satellite imagery, climate, land cover and hazards. Each entry links to the source. For the large datasets shared on Curnagl, see Shared datasets.

Swiss geodata portals ​

UNIL géocatalogueSearch the geodata available at UNIL by region, theme or source. On the Centre informatique wiki, and may require a UNIL login.GlobalUNILUnilGIS geodata serverThe server holding the UNIL geodata: topographic maps, elevation models, aerial and satellite imagery, networks, cadastre, land cover, population and climate models. Access for teaching and research at UNIL, on request.GlobalUNILmap.geo.admin.chThe federal geoportal. Browse federal geodata on a map, see where data exists and download it directly.SwitzerlandOpengeocat.chThe national geodata catalogue. Metadata on datasets covering the whole of Switzerland, physical and human geography alike, and who publishes them.SwitzerlandOpenEnviDatResearch data portal of the Swiss Federal Institute for Forest, Snow and Landscape Research (WSL). Environmental monitoring and research data, with the option to host and publish your own datasets.SwitzerlandOpenopendata.swissThe Swiss open government data portal. Datasets of the Confederation, the cantons and the communes, searchable in one place.SwitzerlandOpendata.geo.admin.chBulk download and STAC API for the federal geodata, the way to script downloads of swissALTI3D or swissSURFACE3D tiles.SwitzerlandOpenMeteoSwiss open dataWeather and climate data released as open government data: ground and atmosphere measurements, climate series, radar and forecasts, with the documentation of the files.SwitzerlandOpenWeatherGLAMOSGlacier Monitoring Switzerland. Mass balance, length and volume change of the Swiss glaciers as CSV files, plus glacier inventories from 1850 to 2016.SwitzerlandOpenGlaciers

Elevation and 3D models of Switzerland ​

The swisstopo height products. All are free to download.

swissALTI3DPrecise digital elevation model of the terrain, without vegetation and buildings. The base layer for GIS, 3D visualisation, hazard and forestry planning, and orthorectification of imagery.SwitzerlandOpenDEMswissSURFACE3DClassified airborne LiDAR point cloud of all natural and built objects on the surface. 3D building models, biomass and canopy models, terrain features under forest cover.SwitzerlandOpenLiDARswissSURFACE3D RasterDigital surface model including vegetation and buildings. Runoff and mass-movement models, solar potential of roofs, line-of-sight analyses, and canopy height when combined with swissALTI3D.SwitzerlandOpenDSMswissBATHY3DElevation model of the Swiss lakebeds. Underwater construction, navigation and environmental studies of lakes.SwitzerlandOpenBathymetryDHM25 and DHM/200mHeight models derived from the 1:25,000 national map, at 25 m and 200 m. Terrain analysis at larger scales, where swissALTI3D is more than needed.SwitzerlandOpenDEM

Satellite imagery ​

Copernicus Data SpaceFree and open access to the Sentinel missions: Sentinel-1 radar, Sentinel-2 optical, Sentinel-3 land and ocean, Sentinel-5P atmospheric composition.GlobalOpenImageryLandsatThe USGS archive of Landsat products, free since the 1970s. Vegetation, moisture and snow indices, land-cover change, glacier retreat, urban growth.GlobalOpenImageryNASA EarthdataNASA's Earth science archive: satellite and airborne remote sensing, field campaigns and model output, searchable with Earthdata Search.GlobalOpenPlanetDaily satellite imagery of the land surface. Forest, land-use, crop and drought monitoring, and change detection at high temporal resolution.GlobalCommercialImagery

Climate and weather ​

Copernicus Climate Data StoreThe catalogue of the Copernicus Climate Change Service: ERA5 and ERA5-Land reanalyses, seasonal forecasts and climate projections, through the web or its API. ERA5 is also kept on Curnagl, see Shared datasets.GlobalOpenClimate

Land cover and statistics ​

ESA CCI Land CoverAnnual global land-cover maps from 1992 to 2020. Land-cover and land-use change at coarse resolution over wide areas.GlobalOpenLand coverCopernicus Land Monitoring ServiceCORINE Land Cover for Europe and the Global Land Service products on vegetation, water cycle, energy budget and cryosphere, with long time series.EuropeGlobalOpenLand coverEurostatStatistical office of the European Union. Geostatistical data for mapping and the analysis of population and human activity across the EU.EuropeOpenStatisticsOpenStreetMapCollaborative, freely editable geographic database of the world. Map production, street-level features and routing.GlobalOpenVector

Hazards and geophysics ​

European Ground Motion ServiceGround-motion velocities at millimetre accuracy over Europe, from radar interferometry. Slow landslides, subsidence, mining and volcanic unrest, as basic, calibrated and ortho products.EuropeOpenInSAREFFIS active fire detectionActive fires located from the thermal anomalies of NASA FIRMS. Fire monitoring, hazard planning and evacuation.EuropeOpenFireSwiss Seismological ServiceThe federal agency for earthquakes, at ETH Zurich. Earthquake catalogue, the seismic hazard model of Switzerland and waveform data through FDSN web services.SwitzerlandOpenSeismicAlpArraySeismic network across the Alps, Apennines, Carpathians and Dinarides. Seismicity and seismic hazard, surface processes, 3D imaging of the orogen and mantle dynamics.EuropeOpenSeismicRaspberry ShakeLow-cost seismometers for professionals, schools and hobbyists. The global network of over 1500 stations streams waveforms in real time.GlobalOpenSeismic

More lists and tools ​

Not catalogues themselves, but useful starting points.

FreeGISDataA categorised list of free GIS datasets, physical, human and by country.GlobalListsGISGeographyTen free GIS data sources, raster and vector, including the Esri open data hub.GlobalListsUniversity of Pittsburgh libraryA library guide of links to geodatabases, GIS software and other resources.GlobalListsHeavy.aiGPU-accelerated analytics and GIS software, an alternative to QGIS for very large datasets.SoftwareGeoDataSolutionsGeodata on demand: cartography, flow modelling, spatial and spatio-temporal analysis, 2D and 3D.SwitzerlandCompanyGEODATAGeodata on demand for mining, tunnelling and infrastructure projects.AustriaCompany
