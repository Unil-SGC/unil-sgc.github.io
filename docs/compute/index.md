# Compute

Where FGSE researchers can run their computations, and who helps them do it.

## Resources

- [Octopus](/compute/octopus): the GPU cluster operated within FGSE. No scheduler, direct access to the nodes. The [how-to](/compute/octopus) covers access, etiquette, SSH and VS Code setup, and programming environments.
- **DCSR HPC clusters**: UNIL's central high-performance computing infrastructure, including a cluster for sensitive data, operated by the [Scientific Computing and Research Support Unit (DCSR)](https://www.unil.ch/ci/dcsr-en). The DCSR also provides virtual workstations and servers for research.
- [CSCS](https://www.cscs.ch): the Swiss National Supercomputing Centre. National resources are available through allocation calls.

## Support

- [Scientific Computing and Research Support Unit, DCSR](https://www.unil.ch/ci/dcsr-en): the DCSR (Division calcul et soutien à la recherche) provides computation and storage resources to the UNIL community as well as transverse expertise to the faculties and affiliated institutes, from high-performance computing (HPC) programming support to full-stack development consulting.

- [HPC support to FGSE researchers](https://wiki.unil.ch/ci/books/research-support/page/high-performance-computing): a team with HPC expertise provides free support to FGSE researchers on a wide range of topics, including parallelisation and optimisation of codes, benchmarking, algorithm development and implementation, and help with computing-time applications to HPC centres such as CSCS. The team members are **Flavio Calvo** and **Margot Sirdey**. They can be reached at **research-computing-fgse@unil.ch** or during the weekly drop-in session (Geopolis 4631, UNIL). Collaborations in mid- and long-term projects are encouraged. Examples of topics:
  - adapting code to the HPC infrastructure (parallelisation, optimisation)
  - advice and help with designing and implementing numerical schemes and algorithms
  - assistance with the DCSR infrastructure: running on the cluster, on virtual machines, data storage
  - automation of data post-processing pipelines
  - refactoring code in view of public sharing

- **Geocomputing Centre**: for questions on FGSE compute resources and coordination, write to sgc@unil.ch.
